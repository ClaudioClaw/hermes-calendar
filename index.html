Hermes Calendar Reader

A private, single-user plugin that reads Google Calendar events.

What it does

Hermes Calendar Reader is a personal assistant plugin. It lets its single operator ask questions about their own calendar — what is scheduled today, when a given event starts, whether a time slot is free — and answers them by reading events from a small, fixed list of Google calendars the operator has explicitly allowed.

How it accesses Google Calendar

  • It requests exactly one scope, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.events.readonly, which is read-only. It cannot create, change, or delete any event.
  • It signs in as one dedicated Google account, and reads only the calendars that have been shared with that account and listed in the plugin's own configuration.
  • It runs on the operator's own machine. There is no server, no shared deployment, and no other user.

Who it is for

One person: its operator. It is not offered to the public, has no sign-up, and is not distributed as a product.
